# Natural Earth land outlines

Public-domain Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, converted to equirectangular SVG paths with three decimal places. Coordinates are x=longitude+180, y=90-latitude.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
